Aggregation Fatigue

The invisible friction of knowledge work.

We now think across AIs.
One question deepens in Claude, another gets structured in ChatGPT,
documents live in Notion, notes in Obsidian,
and fragments scatter across Slack and chat.

But every time we cross a tool, the context is lost.
The same premise, in another chat, on another page —
how many times have we rewritten it?

Without a way to extend Cowan's cognitive limit (4±1)
outside of our own heads,
we keep paying the quietest cost of all:
having the same discussion, from scratch, again.
